How a solar install works with us

Most home systems take four to eight weeks from first visit to switching on. Here is what happens in that time.

  1. 1

    Site visit

    We look at your roof or yard, your panel, and a year of your power bills.

  2. 2

    Design and quote

    You get a system layout, expected yearly production and a fixed price in writing.

  3. 3

    Permits and install

    We handle permits and utility paperwork. Most installs take one to three days on site.

  4. 4

    Switch on and monitor

    We walk you through the monitoring app and check in after your first month.
