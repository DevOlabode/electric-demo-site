Going solar

A typical home system takes four to eight weeks from the first visit to switching it on. Most of that is permits and utility approval. The install itself is one to three days.

  1. Site visit

    We look at your roof or yard, your electrical panel, and twelve months of power bills.

  2. Design and quote

    You get a panel layout, expected production month by month, and a fixed price in writing.

  3. Permits and install

    We handle the permit and the utility paperwork, then book the install around your schedule.

  4. Switch on

    We set up the monitoring app with you and check in after the first month to compare the numbers.

Common questions

Does solar work in Alberta winters?

Yes, though it makes much less in December than in June. Alberta gets more sun than most of Canada, and the credits from summer production help cover winter bills.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. Plenty of 100 amp homes can run a Level 2 charger after a load calculation, or with a load management device. We check before recommending anything.

Who looks after permits and inspections?

We do. Permits, utility paperwork and booking the inspection are part of the quote.

How soon can you come out?

Usually within a week for a quote visit. Urgent repairs are often same day or next day.
