Meet the owner

Dale Kowalchuk is a Master Electrician who started Prairie Current in 2009 with one truck and mostly farm calls around Fort Saskatchewan.

Solar came later, after a customer with a long acreage driveway asked whether panels could cover the shop. Dale took the training, did that job, and the work grew from there.

Today the company is four electricians and three trucks, and it is still family-run. Dale still looks over every quote before it goes out.
